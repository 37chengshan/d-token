## Evidence hygiene

- Every artifact above was consumed from an explicit path manifest; no
  mtime/latest/directory-scan selection was used.
- Every envelope's `artifact_sha256` was recomputed (remove field → canonical
  JSON → SHA-256) and compared exactly.
- Missing, stale (started before the N0 freeze finished), old-candidate,
  digest-mismatch, dirty, or pass-only evidence is rejected — none of it
  appears in this report.
- Raw prompts, responses, PNGs, base64, secrets, auth material, and absolute
  config paths never enter evidence artifacts.
